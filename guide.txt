Here's a quick guide to using **ThirdEye-phone.apk**.

## 1. Install
1. **Copy** `apk/ThirdEye-phone.apk` to your Android phone (Android 7 or newer), by USB, Google Drive or WhatsApp-to-yourself.
2. **Tap** the file and allow installs from that app when asked.
3. **If Play Protect warns** about an unknown app, tap **Install anyway**.

## 2. First launch
- The yellow splash screen shows, then Home, and the app says hello.
- When a feature is first used, Android asks for permissions: **allow** microphone, camera, location, contacts, phone and SMS.

## 3. Home screen

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Big yellow "Tap to speak" button** (bottom) | Wait for "Speak now", then say a command |
| **What is this?** | Takes a photo and describes it ("This looks like a cup…") |
| **Money** | Opens the note reader |
| **Read text** / **Read display** | Opens the text reader. Hold the page up, then press the big yellow **Take photo** button |
| **Around me** | Live camera that announces objects and where they are ("person ahead, close") |
| **Messages** | Reads your new SMS aloud |

## 4. Voice commands (examples)
- "**What is this?**", "**What note is this?**", "**Read this**", "**Read in Sinhala**"
- "**What's around me?**"
- "**Read my messages**", "**Send a message to Amma**", "**WhatsApp Kamal**"
- "**Call Amma**", "**Take me to Kandy**"
- "**What's the weather?**", "**Torch on**", "**Torch off**"
- "**Speak Sinhala**", "**Speak Tamil**", "**Speak English**" (switches the app's language)
- "**Help**" (lists the commands aloud)

When sending a message, the app asks "Who…?" and "What should it say?", reads the message back, then waits for **yes** or **no**.

## 5. On camera screens
- **Back** closes the screen and returns to Home.
- On the "What is this?" screen, **tap anywhere** to check again, or use the **✕** at the top right.
- Hold the phone steady, about an arm's length away, in good light.

To enable the ⚠️ items:
1. Download `efficientdet_lite2.tflite` (detector) and the Tesseract language files into `app/src/main/assets/`.
2. Rebuild with `.\gradlew assemblePhoneDebug`.
3. Install the new APK from `app/build/outputs/apk/phone/debug/`.

The download commands are in my earlier messages, and I can do the rebuild for you.

## 7. If something goes wrong
- **"App not installed"**: uninstall the old Third Eye first, then install again.
- **No reaction to voice**: check the phone has internet, or install the Google app for speech recognition.
- **No speech at all**: check the media volume, and that a voice is installed under Settings → Text-to-speech.
